About Access Astrolabe

Access Astrolabe helps developers inspect and understand accessibility behavior directly in the browser. Simulate screen reader navigation, inspect computed accessibility information, and trace how accessible names are constructed. Designed for debugging, learning, and smoke-testing during development.
